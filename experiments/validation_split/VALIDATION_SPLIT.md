# Validation split — construction and statistics

Generated by `src/build_validation_split.py` (module `src/ber/validation_split.py`). Uses training data only; the test files are never read.

## Construction

- **Unit of splitting: Source-1 entity.** Fraction 1/5 to validation, seed `20260925`.
- S1 strata: country x min(n_true_matches, 8) x (has_S2_match, has_S3_match) (38 non-empty strata). Largest per-stratum deviation from exact 20%: 0.60 entities.
- Ordering inside a stratum: blake2b('<seed>:<entity_id>', digest_size=8), ascending, within stratum; quotas: floor(n/5) + largest remainder; total = round(N/5).
- True targets (S2/S3 records in the ground truth) inherit the fold of their S1 entity (each target belongs to exactly one S1 — re-checked at build time).
- Distractors (S2/S3 records matched to no S1) are split 20/80 with strata source x country.
- Result: every S1 and every S2/S3 record is in exactly one fold; fit and validation pools are disjoint.
- Validation files: `val_as_test_dir/test_source{1,2,3}.tsv` (dataset/test layout, training rows only) and `val_ground_truth.tsv`. Fit fold: `assignment/*_fold.npy` (0 = fit, 1 = val per train-file row) + `ber.validation_split.iter_fold_lines`.

## Known limitation

The validation pool reproduces the TRAIN composition (4.68 S2+S3 records per S1, ~26% distractors). The test set has 5.75 S2+S3 records per S1; whether that comes from more matches per S1 or more distractors is unknowable without test labels, so validation scores may be optimistic on precision.
